Tool 1 of 6

ROI vs Residual Income Comparator

ROI is a percentage -- operating income divided by investment. Residual income is a dollar amount -- operating income minus a capital charge. The two metrics can lead to different decisions: managers focused on ROI may reject projects that would increase corporate value but lower their division's ROI.

Tool 2 of 6

EVA Calculator

Economic Value Added refines residual income by using net operating profit after tax (NOPAT) and the weighted-average cost of capital (WACC). It also adjusts the investment base by adding back certain capitalized items. EVA is often considered the truest measure of value creation.

Tool 3 of 6

DuPont Decomposition

ROI breaks down into Profit Margin times Asset Turnover. Two divisions can have identical ROI but get there through completely different strategies -- one through high margins and slow turnover (luxury goods), another through low margins and fast turnover (grocery). Compare two divisions or two time periods.

Multinational Performance Comparator

When two subsidiaries operate in different countries, raw financial comparisons can mislead. Currency exchange rates, inflation differences, and different economic conditions all affect reported performance. Adjust for these factors before judging which manager is performing better.
