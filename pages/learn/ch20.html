Tool 4 of 5

Manufacturing Cycle Efficiency (MCE) Calculator

Manufacturing cycle time is the total time from when an order is received until it is delivered. Only processing time adds value. Inspection, waiting, and moving are non-value-added activities. MCE = Processing Time / Manufacturing Cycle Time. A perfect MCE of 1.0 means zero waste.

The Pareto principle states that roughly 80% of defects come from 20% of causes. Enter up to 8 defect types with their frequencies. The diagram ranks them from most to least frequent and overlays a cumulative percentage line so you can identify which defects to address first.
