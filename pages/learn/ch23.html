Tool 2 of 4

Transfer Price Method Comparator

Three primary methods exist: market-based uses the external market price; cost-based uses variable cost, full cost, or cost-plus markup; negotiated allows divisions to bargain. Each produces different transfer prices and different divisional profit splits, but corporate profit is the same.

Tool 3 of 4

General Transfer Pricing Rule

The general rule says: minimum transfer price = incremental cost per unit + opportunity cost per unit. When the selling division has idle capacity, opportunity cost is zero, so the minimum equals variable cost. When at full capacity, opportunity cost equals the contribution margin lost on the next-best external sale.

Tool 4 of 4

Multinational Tax Strategy

When divisions are in different countries with different tax rates, transfer pricing affects global tax liability. The strategy is to recognize profit in the lower-tax jurisdiction -- but the transfer price must stay within an arms-length range allowed by tax authorities. Compare strategies and see the global tax impact.
