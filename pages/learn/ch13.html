Strategy, Balanced Scorecard, and Strategic Profitability Analysis

Evaluate whether a company pursues product differentiation or cost leadership, build a balanced scorecard across four perspectives, and decompose operating-income changes into growth, price-recovery, and productivity components.

Learning Objectives

  1. Recognize which of two generic strategies a company is using
  2. Understand the four perspectives of the balanced scorecard and how they link via a strategy map
  3. Analyze changes in operating income to evaluate strategy using growth, price-recovery, and productivity components
  4. Distinguish engineered from discretionary costs and identify unused capacity and downsizing decisions
