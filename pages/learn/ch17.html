Cost Allocation: Joint Products and Byproducts

Allocate joint costs across main products using sales value, NRV, and constant gross margin methods, evaluate sell-or-process-further decisions, and compare byproduct accounting approaches.

Learning Objectives

  1. Identify the splitoff point in a joint-cost situation
  2. Distinguish joint products from byproducts
  3. Explain why joint costs are irrelevant in sell-or-process-further decisions
  4. Allocate joint costs using the sales-value-at-splitoff, NRV, and constant gross margin percentage NRV methods
  5. Account for byproducts using the production method and the sales method

Byproduct Accounting Comparator
