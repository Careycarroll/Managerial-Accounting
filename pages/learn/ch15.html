Customer Profitability Chapter 15

Cost Allocation and Customer Profitability Analysis

Trace and allocate customer-related costs, analyze customer profitability, visualize profit concentration with a whale curve, and decompose sales-volume performance into mix, quantity, market-share, and market-size effects.

Not started

Learning Objectives

  1. Identify the purposes and criteria used to allocate costs to customers and channels
  2. Classify customer costs using the customer cost hierarchy
  3. Prepare and interpret customer profitability reports
  4. Use whale-curve analysis to understand profit concentration and customer tiers
  5. Calculate sales-volume, sales-mix, sales-quantity, market-share, and market-size variances
