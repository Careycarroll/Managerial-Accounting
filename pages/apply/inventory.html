📦 Business Scenario

Your warehouse manager orders 2,000 units at a time. Annual demand is 24,000 units. Each order costs $150 to place. Carrying cost is $8 per unit per year. Average daily demand is 66 units, max daily demand is 90 units, average lead time is 5 days, and max lead time is 8 days. Is this the right order quantity?

Concept

EOQ and Reorder Point

The economic order quantity minimizes total inventory costs. The reorder point tells you when to place the next order. Safety stock protects against demand spikes and supplier delays.
