🏢 Business Scenario

Your plant manager wants to replace aging equipment with a new machine costing $500,000. It will reduce labor and material costs by $120,000 per year over a 6-year life, with a salvage value of $50,000. Your company requires a 12% return and pays 30% tax. Is this investment worth it?

Concept

NPV, Payback & IRR

Enter the key parameters of a capital project. NPV tells you whether the investment creates value at your required rate of return. Payback shows how quickly you recover your cash. IRR is the rate that makes NPV zero.
