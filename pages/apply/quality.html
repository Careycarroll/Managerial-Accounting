Your plant spends $40,000 on prevention and $25,000 on appraisal, but incurs $80,000 in internal failures and $120,000 in external failures on $2,000,000 in revenue. The industry benchmark is 4% of revenue. Where should you focus your quality investment?

Concept

Cost-of-Quality Report

Quality costs fall into four categories: prevention, appraisal, internal failure, and external failure. The first two are costs of conformance -- investments to avoid defects. The last two are costs of nonconformance -- what you pay when quality fails.
