Should we make it or buy it?

Compare the relevant costs of producing in-house versus outsourcing. Factor in opportunity costs, avoidable fixed costs, and qualitative considerations.

🏭 Business Scenario

Your factory makes a component used in your main product. You produce 50,000 units per year. Variable cost is $38 per unit, avoidable fixed costs are $400,000, and unavoidable fixed costs are $600,000. A supplier offers to sell the component for $58 per unit. If you outsource, the freed capacity could generate $300,000 in operating income from an alternative use. Should you make or buy?

Concept

Make vs Buy Decision

Only relevant costs matter. Unavoidable fixed costs are the same whether you make or buy -- they are irrelevant. The opportunity cost of freed capacity is relevant if you have a better use for it.

Analysis

Full Relevant Cost Analysis

Break down the make cost into its components. Find the indifference price -- the outside price at which you are exactly neutral. Evaluate qualitative factors. If buying from a sister division, apply the general transfer pricing rule.
