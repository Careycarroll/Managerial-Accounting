🏢 Business Scenario

Your division reports $2,000,000 operating income on $10,000,000 in assets. The company requires a 15% return and your cost of capital is 12%. Is the division creating or destroying value?

Concept

ROI, Residual Income & EVA

ROI measures return as a percentage of assets. Residual income shows the dollar value created above the hurdle rate. EVA adjusts for the true cost of capital. Together they reveal whether a division is genuinely creating value.
