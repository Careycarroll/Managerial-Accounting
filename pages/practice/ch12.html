Relevant Costs

Randomized multi-step decision problems. Each scenario embeds the data in a short business case — extract what matters, run the relevant-cost analysis, then commit to a decision.

Choose a problem type. Each problem mixes calculation steps with accept/reject or keep/drop decisions, with randomized numbers every attempt. Show-work is available on demand after each submission.
